Our Story

Hamirpur's First Themed Restaurant

Born from a love of flavour and a passion for creating experiences, Cafe Petoo Adda is more than a restaurant — it's your adda, your hangout, your place. We proudly serve the city's most-loved food with top-notch quality, bringing Kanpur-level vibes straight to Hamirpur.

Whether you're dining in with family, catching up with friends over pizza, or grabbing a quick bite on the go — our themed ambience and flavour-packed menu make every visit unforgettable. As a women-owned business, we take extra pride in every dish we serve.
